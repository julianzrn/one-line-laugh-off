## Why the selected candidate wins

It lands on a cold read with zero setup: a wellness trip, taken to learn self-care, is exactly what killed the one living thing in the narrator's care. The turn is a single quiet irony — the self-improvement and the casualty are the same event — and it needs no punchline word bolted on, because the last phrase quietly convicts the narrator. At 84 characters it is the most economical of the eight, and the joke is a fresh construction rather than a recycled format.
